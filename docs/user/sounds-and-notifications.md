# Sounds and notifications

T3 Code plays short synthesized sound effects for agent activity, and can show an OS notification
banner when something needs you while the window is out of focus.

The sounds are:

- **Agent finished** — a turn completed.
- **Agent needs input** — an agent is waiting on your answer or approval.
- **Agent error** — a session failed.
- **Button clicks** — a quiet tick on every button press.

To change them:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Sound**.
3. Toggle sounds overall, adjust the volume, or toggle each event individually. The play button
   next to each event previews it.

Notification banners fire only while the window is unfocused or minimized — when you are looking at
the app, the sounds alone signal events. If your browser has not granted notification permission
yet, Settings shows an **Enable notifications** button.

Sound preferences are stored per device: connecting from another computer or phone keeps its own
choices.
